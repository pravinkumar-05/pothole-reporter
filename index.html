About RoadGuard Maharashtra

RoadGuard Maharashtra is a citizen-driven initiative by the Government of Maharashtra under the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) program. Our mission is to create safer roads by empowering citizens to report road damage quickly and efficiently.

Every complaint is reviewed by the Public Works Department (PWD) and routed to the appropriate municipal authority for timely repair. Citizens can track the progress of their complaint in real-time.
