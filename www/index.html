Government of Maharashtra • Public Works Department (PWD)

Institutional Road Safety & Citizen Infrastructure Network

RoadGuard Maharashtra is an enterprise civic infrastructure reporting platform built to empower citizens, municipal corporations, and the Maharashtra Public Works Department. By bridging high-precision GIS telemetry, real-time photographic evidence verification, and automated municipal routing, RoadGuard accelerates asphalt repair across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.

36 Districts Covered
350+ PWD Wards Integrated
72h Field Response SLA
100% Geotagged Proof

Core Operational Pillars

AI & Photo Verification

Every report requires live photographic evidence with high-precision timestamping and road surface distress validation to eliminate false complaints.

Sub-Meter GIS Telemetry

Automated spatial coordinates pinpoint exact potholes on Maharashtra's arterial highways and city roads, routing tickets directly to the responsible division.

Real-Time Citizen Tracking

Citizens receive transparent tracking from the moment a pothole is pinned through PWD inspection, asphalt patch repair, and resolution verification.

Institutional Accountability

Built under the Smart India Hackathon initiative in direct alignment with Maharashtra PWD standards, urban governance, and RTI transparency protocols.
